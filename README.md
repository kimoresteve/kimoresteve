## 👨‍💻 About Me
 
I build software that handles real money, real messages and real customers. My background runs from M-Pesa and banking integrations at scale to shipping my own multi-tenant SaaS products as a one-person team.
 
I have spent years designing multi-tenant architecture with Stancl Tenancy, wiring up payment and messaging APIs, and taking products from idea to production on my own.
 
---
 
## 🚀 What I've Built
 
### 💬 BizPlusX
**Multi-tenant WhatsApp Business platform | Meta Tech Provider**
 
A SaaS platform that lets businesses run customer conversations, automation and billing on the WhatsApp Cloud API. I designed the multi-tenant architecture, automation engine and billing, and I keep expanding it to new channels.
 
`Laravel` `Vue` `WhatsApp Cloud API` `Multi-tenancy` `Docker`
 
### 🏠 Texkin
**Rental property management for Kenyan landlords**
 
A platform that helps landlords manage properties, tenants, payments and penalties without spreadsheets. It includes a web dashboard, a penalty ledger, flexible pricing and a Flutter frontend.
 
`Laravel` `Vue` `Flutter` `M-Pesa / Daraja` `MySQL`
 
### 💸 WaftPay
**Android remittance app**
 
A mobile money transfer app built for Android. I worked on it as Senior Mobile Developer and carried that experience into my own products.
 
`Kotlin` `Android` `Payments`
 
### 🧾 POS and CRM Apps
Flutter-based point of sale and CRM apps for small businesses, built for real shop floors with real constraints.
 
`Flutter` `Dart` `Offline-first thinking`
